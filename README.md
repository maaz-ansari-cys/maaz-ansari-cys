### About


I'm a **Cybersecurity practitioner** focused on building practical experience across defensive security, digital forensics, and network security.

I've developed hands on experience through a **SOC internship, CTFs, university coursework, and self-driven labs**, working with **Wazuh SIEM, pfSense, network traffic analysis, Windows/Linux environments, and digital forensics**. I've also worked on projects involving forensic analysis, security monitoring, and web security.

Currently, I'm strengthening my foundations in **SOC operations, DFIR, and security monitoring**, while exploring **offensive security and cloud ** to better understand attacks from a defensive perspective.

I prefer learning by **building, testing, troubleshooting, and documenting what I actually do**.
